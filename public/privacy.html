Privacy Policy

Bridge Scorer by Athelite — last updated 2026-08-14

In short: Bridge Scorer collects no personal data. Nothing you enter ever leaves your device.

What the app stores

Team names, player names, deal history, and your scoring-rule preferences are saved only in your browser or device's local storage. This data never leaves your device — it is not sent to Athelite, to any server, or to any third party. There is no account, no sign-in, and no backend service involved.

Analytics and tracking

The app includes no analytics, advertising, or tracking software of any kind.

Sharing your scorecard

If you use the app's own "share" or "export" feature, the plain-text scorecard is handed to your device's native share sheet or downloaded as a file — an action you take deliberately, using your platform's own tools. Athelite never sees or receives that data.

Permissions

The Android app requests no special device permissions. It displays the same web app you can use directly in a browser.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the update will be posted on this page.
